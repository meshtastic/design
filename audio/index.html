Chirpy Audio & Ringtone Suite

Custom, friendly sounds for Meshtastic. Inspired by mascot Chirpy (the anthropomorphic radio node) and the distinctive //\ (Up, Up, Down) pattern of the Meshtastic logo and LoRa CSS radio syncwords.

The //\ Motif: Mascot, Logo, and LoRa Physics

Chirpy is Meshtastic's mascot: an anthropomorphic radio node (the cute green node with an antenna, OLED screen eyes, hands, and the //\ logo on his chest). His name and voice originate directly from LoRa Chirp Spread Spectrum (CSS) radio modulation. In LoRa physics, packet headers start with ascending frequency sweeps (upchirps /) followed by framing downchirps (\). The Chirpy sound suite translates this visual logo and electromagnetic signature into a friendly, tactile electronic vocalization.

/ Stroke 1
First Upchirp (C#6 → F#6). Greeting chirrup.
/ Stroke 2
Second Upchirp (A#6 → F#7). Climbing to summit peak.
\ Stroke 3
Downchirp (F#7 → C#7 → F#6). Warm cascade settling at home.

Chirpy //\ (Notification Sound)

Flagship Notification

The official Meshtastic notification sound. A friendly electronic vocalization from Chirpy the node, embodying the //\ logo and LoRa syncword. Tactile CSS sweeps layered over acoustic bell chimes for phone apps, and a crisp 3-burst arpeggio on hardware node buzzers.
