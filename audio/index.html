Meshtastic Design

Chirpy notification sounds

Sounds for the Meshtastic apps and devices, named for Chirpy. Each sound is one RTTTL string. The phone files and the buzzer previews are rendered from it.

The logo shape in chirpy

The mark is a / on its own, then a joined /\. All three strokes are the same height. The sweeps are straight lines in frequency, like LoRa chirps.

/
F#6 up to F#7, then a short gap
/\
F#6 up to F#7 and straight back down to F#6, with no gap at the peak
