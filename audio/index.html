Meshtastic Design

Chirpy notification sounds

Sounds for the Meshtastic apps and devices, named for Chirpy and shaped like the //\ logo: two rising strokes, then a falling one. Each sound is one RTTTL string. The phone files and the buzzer previews are rendered from it.
